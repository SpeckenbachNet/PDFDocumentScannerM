# Pages

The list on the left holds the scanned pages, numbered in their order. The page you click is shown large on the right.

## Selecting

A click selects a page. Hold Ctrl or Shift to select several pages; Ctrl+A selects all. The arrow keys move through the list.

## Removing

- Rest the mouse on a page and an **×** appears at its top right; a click on it removes the page.
- The Delete or Backspace key removes the selected pages.
- Right-click a page and choose **Remove page** (or **Remove n pages** for a selection).

> Ctrl+Z brings a removed page back (see Undo).

## Reordering

Drag a page to its new place. Near the top or bottom edge of the list it scrolls by itself. The pages are numbered again afterwards.

## Rotating

The button with the round arrow rotates the selected pages by 90° clockwise. Click it twice for upside down, three times for 90° to the left.

## Cropping

For a small slip on the flatbed, such as a till receipt: the page then is only as large as the slip.

1. Select the page and click the crop button next to the rotate button. The preview shows a frame, everything outside it is darkened. The frame already lies around the content of the page.
2. Drag a corner or an edge of the frame to change it, drag inside it to move it. Dragging outside the frame draws a new one. Zoom works as usual, so you can work exactly.
3. **Apply** (or Enter) cuts the page to the frame. **Cancel**, Esc or another click on the crop button leaves the page as it is.

> Cropped wrongly? Ctrl+Z brings the whole page back.

## Clearing

**Clear** removes all pages. PDFDocumentScannerM asks first; Ctrl+Z brings the pages back.

## Undo

**Ctrl+Z** undoes the last step, **Ctrl+Shift+Z** redoes it. Both are also in the right-click menu of the page list. Undo works for removing, clearing, reordering, rotating and cropping, many steps back, until you close PDFDocumentScannerM. Newly scanned pages are no step; just remove them.

## Preview and zoom

The large preview shows the whole page and grows with the window.

- **Zoom:** Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms at the mouse. Ctrl + Plus and Ctrl + Minus zoom in and out, Ctrl + 0 shows the whole page again.
- **Double click:** switches between the whole page and 100 %.
- **Zoom bar:** while the mouse is over the preview, **−**, the zoom level and **+** appear at the bottom right. A click on the zoom level shows the whole page.
- **Moving:** when zoomed, drag the page with the mouse or scroll with the mouse wheel.
